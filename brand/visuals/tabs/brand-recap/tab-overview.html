§1 · What is Roxabi
Open-source primitives that compound

Roxabi ships open-source primitives — routing, state, orchestration, agent harnesses, tool connectors. Each primitive works standalone. Together they give a solo developer or small team a full-stack foundation that a 50-person org would otherwise build internally. Open by architecture: inspectable, forkable, no telemetry, yours to own the moment you clone it.

Mission
Give a solo developer or small team the same composable foundation a 50-person engineering org has — open-source pieces that compound, pre-wired, theirs to own.
Category
Open-source compounding primitives. Each one works standalone, builds on the others, and belongs to the builder the moment they fork it.
Scope note
Agents are one kind of primitive — not the defining category. Copy reflects the breadth; agent use cases lead as the primary example without becoming the only noun.
