§2.4 · The 50-person org framing

The gap between what one person can build and what a full engineering team can build is structural, not a matter of skill. A team has someone dedicated to the orchestration layer. A platform person. Someone who owns the state layer. The solo developer has to build that from scratch before the interesting work starts — or buy something closed and end up on someone else's roadmap.

Roxabi closes the gap by making the foundations available as open infrastructure you own. You start where the team starts. The interesting part starts on day one.

The comparison is structural (gap narrows) — never a claim of equivalence. Say "the structural disadvantage of building foundations from scratch is removed." Never say "as good as a 50-person team."
