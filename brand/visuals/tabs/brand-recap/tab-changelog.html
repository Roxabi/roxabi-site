Brand book version trail — v1.0 → v1.5

Decision trail for the Roxabi brand book. v1.5 is the current authoritative version as of 2026-04-20. The dominant migration in v1.5 is the palette ground shift: warm-dark → cool-dark, with amber kept and warm text retained against the cool ground as a deliberate signature.

§7 · Version timeline
v1.5 · 2026-04-20 · current
Cool-dark palette migration

Ground shift: warm-dark family (#111210, #1a1b18, #2e3028) retired in favour of cool-dark (#0d1117, #13191f, #161b22). Amber #f0b429 kept across the migration. Warm off-white text #f0ede6 retained against the cool ground as a deliberate signature. Border pair migrated to #21262d / #30363d. Everything else — positioning, voice, personas, messaging, metaphors — unchanged from v1.4.

v1.1 – v1.4 · process revisions
Logo exploration unlocked, process refinements

v1.1 process note: the v1.0 logo description ("Geometric R + amber slash") was generated as a placeholder during brand exploration — never actually decided. Logo mark reset to "in exploration, not locked." Color system, typography, and visual language remained locked. Other revisions tightened the vocabulary tables and the three-layer framing with Mickael's personal book.

v1.0 · initial authoritative
Brand book established — single source of truth

Replaced exploration artifacts (phase 1–4 HTML docs) as the single source of truth. Locked: positioning statement, 50-person org framing, four personas with priority order, five voice attributes + ten writing rules + vocabulary guide, four messaging pillars, two narrative arcs, three core metaphors. Locked color system used warm-dark ground with amber accent — subsequently migrated in v1.5.
