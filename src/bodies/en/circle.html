Positioning

What it is — and what it is not

Is

A closed circle of builders

AI as subject or tool. Open source as practice. People who publish public code, argue stack choices, and share harnesses that actually run.

Is not

A generic AI Discord

Not a course, not a job board, not crypto, not no-code support. If the room rewards noise, the filter failed — and we tighten it.

Entry

GitHub is the door

Entry is filtered on purpose. The goal is density: fewer tourists, harder technical threads, peers who already ship in the open.

01

Join Discord

Start on the server. The invite is public; membership inside the circle is not automatic.

02

Prove the profile

GitHub identity + an entry PR on the applications repo. Public signal, not a vibe check.

03

Score, then role

Heuristic scoring on public activity. Threshold met → member. Below → numeric feedback; the algo is open.

04

Appeal when edge

Private-only history or borderline cases go through appeal. Staff can override. Re-apply after cooldown.
